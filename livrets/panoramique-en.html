A few guidelines to make sure everything goes smoothly.

The apartment is entirely non-smoking. The CO₂ sensor must not be moved: it will trigger an alarm.

Please respect this rule out of consideration for future guests with allergies.
🎉
No parties
A decibel detector and a camera on the landing monitor noise levels. Please respect your neighbours.

Quiet after 10pm
The building is residential: please keep noise down after 10pm.
👥
Registered guests
Only the people listed on your booking are authorised. If there are any changes, please update your reservation.
⚙️How it works
Wifi
SFR BAC8 network
Television
SFR Box + apps (Netflix…)
Kitchen
See equipment
Induction hob (Ikea). The two left burners display a flashing « U » if your pan is too small: use larger pans or the right burner, which works with smaller cookware.Microwave, oven, dishwasher (tablets provided), kettle, and everything you need (pans, skillets, utensils).
Coffee
Nespresso machine — capsules provided on first night
Sofa bed
Where are the sheets?
A sofa bed is available in the living room. Sheets are in the cupboard under the TV, and duvets in the sofa's storage compartment. Sleeping arrangements: 2 double beds (160×200) + the sofa bed. A travel cot is available on request before arrival.
Washer-dryer
Details
The washing machine in the kitchen has a drying function. It automatically shuts off from 8 pm to 8 am to keep the peace for neighbors (a child's bedroom is directly below). A second Samsung tumble dryer with condensation (16 kg) is provided for cleaning, but feel free to use it if needed.
Heating & air conditioning
How it works
Winter — radiators with thermostat set to 20 °C (automatic cutoff above that). Off in summer. Summer — portable air conditioning unit on the mezzanine. For best performance: lower the Velux blinds, open opposite windows to create airflow, and give the unit time to cool things down.
Electrical panel
In case of power cut
The electrical panel is on the landing, outside the apartment, in the cupboard on the left. Check that all small switches are on and the main round button is set to 1. Unplug large appliances before resetting if it trips again.
